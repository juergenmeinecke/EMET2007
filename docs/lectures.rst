Lecture Slides
==============

.. note::

    All readings refer to the Stock and Watson textbook *Introduction to Econometrics*. You can use the
    *updated 3rd edition* or the *4th edition*.

    For weeks 1 through 9 the two editions do not differ with regard to textbook references. Beginning
    in week 10 (time series analysis) the updated 3rd edition and the 4th edition have diverging
    chapter numbers (but there is no substantive difference between the two editions other than the
    chapter numbering).


.. admonition:: Acknowledgement

    I am grateful to Alex Looi for his talented and diligent contribution to the lecture notes. Alex
    has written all the Python code throughout.
