TutorBot
======================

I've created a dedicated EMET2007 TutorBot for you. Check in the lower right hand corner of this
website. 

I've fed it all the course material, computer codes and course logistics so that it should do
a decent job at answering your most elementary questions.

Try it out!

.. admonition:: TutorBot Best Practices

   Please observe the following common sense guidelines when interacting with the bot:

   - **It's a study aid, not an answer key**: use it to understand concepts, not to get solutions
   - **Treat everything you type as public**: don't share personal information, passwords, or student IDs
   - **It often makes mistakes**: verify anything critical against lecture notes or talk to us
   - **It won't do your work for you**: it's designed to guide you through problems, not solve them    

   To seek answers on your serious econometrics questions, please reach out to the teaching team.
   We're there to help you!
